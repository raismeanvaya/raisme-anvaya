About Raisme Anvaya

RAISME ANVAYA is a multidisciplinary student club that connects mechanical engineering with robotics and artificial intelligence. Members learn by building, competing, organising and serving the community together. Students from all branches are welcome.

  1. Our vision

    A campus where students from every discipline create technology that improves lives.

  2. Our mission

    Give students hands-on platforms to learn, lead and innovate beyond the classroom.

  3. Our objectives

    Run workshops and competitions, support student projects, and build teamwork, leadership and social responsibility.

  4. Why join

    Work on real projects, meet mentors and peers, join events, and build a portfolio that shows what you can do.

  5. Multidisciplinary approach

    Mechanical design, intelligent software, sport and culture meet in one community, so ideas cross boundaries.
